# Captions, hooks and DM

## Caption formula

1. **Hook line** (≤ 125 chars, shows before "…more"): one of the types below + 1–2 emoji.
2. **Data line**: the real numbers that make the hook true (planned vs done TSS, form value, days to race).
3. **AI-coach turn**: "My AI coach caught it. It reads every ride from Garmin + Strava on intervals.icu…" + its verdict.
4. **Payoff**: where it's heading (form +14 = sweet spot) or the result, with 🎯/📈.
5. **CTA**: "💬 Comment COACH and I'll DM you my exact setup."
6. **Tease** (only if it will be posted): "Race report after Saturday. Did the AI save my race? 👀"
7. **Disclosure**: "🤖 AI-generated voiceover · 🎥 <what/when the footage is>"
8. **Hashtags** (10–15): #cycling #intervalsicu #garmin #strava #aicoach #taper #masterscycling
   #powermeter #roadcycling #omancycling #cyclingoman #trainingdata (+ topic tags).
9. **Before you post** checklist (see SKILL.md §6).

## Hook types (give options across types when asked for "10 captions")

- **Confession**: "Last week was meant to be my taper… I rode DOUBLE 😬"
- **POV**: "POV: you planned a quick, easy spin… and ended up on a long ride in the Oman heat 🥵🚴‍♂️"
- **Number shock**: "381 TSS in a taper week. My AI coach was not impressed."
- **Question**: "Can an AI coach save a race 5 days out?"
- **Contrarian**: "Rest made me faster than any interval session this month."
- **Flip / result**: "Form −39 → +14 in 9 days. Here's what my AI said."

## Fixing a caption someone wrote

- Keep their voice and joke; fix grammar and rhythm ("planning for" → "planned", cut filler).
- Check every claim against data and the calendar: e.g. 30 Oct in Oman is not "the peak of
  summer" → "in the Oman heat". Flag anything you can't verify (ride duration, distance) and
  offer to pull it instead of guessing.
- Return one main version + 3–4 variants + the full caption around it.
- POV grammar: "POV: you …" (second person, present/past simple), not "you're planning … but ended up".
